## The idea

### From the brief

Simon Says with the group's gestures (high five, the meme, the usual shout). The sequence grows one step per round, up to 6.

### What we agreed

- **What the player sees:** a classic Simon board: four big coloured buttons, each with the drawing of one of the group's gestures (high five, the meme, the usual shout…), lighting up in order. The friends appear small at the side.
- **What the player does:** watch the buttons light up in order (each gesture with its drawing and word: HIGH FIVE, AYYY!…), then repeat the sequence by clicking. It starts with 2 gestures and adds one per round, up to 6. The buttons swap places every round, so you must remember the gesture, not where it was.
- **The social twist:** halfway through, a friend makes up a brand-new gesture: a fifth button appears, and from then on the sequence can include it. Rituals are the group's own, and they grow.
- **How you win:** repeat the last sequence, the one with 6 gestures; the whole group does the ritual together.
- **How you lose:** 3 hearts. A mistake costs a heart and the group shows you the same sequence again; with no hearts left you lose ("You forgot the ritual…").
- **How long it lasts:** no clock: 5 rounds (2 to 6 gestures), about 40–60 seconds.

## Decisions

- 01/11/2026: a classic Simon board with four gesture buttons, friends small at the side (rejected: the friends acting out the gestures in their hangout).
- 01/11/2026: the buttons shuffle places every round; you remember gestures, not positions (rejected: buttons always in the same place, with arrow keys).
- 01/11/2026: social twist: a friend invents a new gesture halfway and a fifth button appears (rejected: a new friend learning the ritual from you).
- 01/11/2026: 3 hearts; a mistake replays the same sequence (rejected: one mistake and you're out).
