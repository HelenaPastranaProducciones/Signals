## The idea

### From the brief

"Most Likely To" questions between ways of communicating (call, letter, story reaction, DM, smoke signals). You see what the others voted and get your social level.

### What we agreed

- **What the player sees:** A party-game card on a painted table, with your face and four friends' faces on top. Each card is a small situation with a friend ("Your friend had a terrible day") and four ways to answer, from a quiet like to showing up in person.
- **What the player does:** Clicks how they would answer, within 5 seconds. Then the friends' votes appear next to the answers: everyone connects in their own way, there is no wrong answer.
- **The social twist:** The last question is "Most likely to be there when it matters?" with everyone's faces as answers, and all the friends vote for you. Then you get your social level (The Quiet Watcher, The Low-Key Texter, The Voice Note Person or The Shows-Up Friend) from how you answered.
- **How you win:** Answer all 6 questions.
- **How you lose:** Let the time run out on 2 questions.
- **How long it lasts:** About 30 seconds (5 seconds per question).
