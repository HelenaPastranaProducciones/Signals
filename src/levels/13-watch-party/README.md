## The idea

### From the brief

Split screen with two living rooms (Barcelona and Tokyo) watching the same thing. When something happens, tap both sides within half a second. 8 syncs out of 10.

### What we agreed

- **What the player sees:** A split screen: your room on the left and a friend's room in another city on the right (Tokyo, Seoul, Lagos…, with their local time), both watching the same made-up show. A little chat between the two rooms. Every attempt brings another show, city, friend and moments.
- **What the player does:** When something big happens on a screen ("THE TWIST!", "GOAL!"), clicks that screen, or presses A (yours) and L (theirs). Both screens for every moment = you reacted together.
- **The social twist:** Halfway, the friend's wifi starts to lag ("wait my wifi is dying, don't spoil it"): their screen shows each moment a bit later. Clicking their side before it appears there is a spoiler and counts as a miss: you have to wait for them.
- **How you win:** React together to 8 of the 10 moments.
- **How you lose:** Miss (or spoil) 3 moments.
- **How long it lasts:** About 40 seconds.

## Decisions

- 01/11/2026: Helena approved the idea proposed by Claude (split screen, same show, the friend's connection lags halfway) and asked to build it straight away. The time to react together is 1.4 seconds, a bit more than the brief's 0.5, so it can be played with a mouse.
