## The idea

### From the brief

Two images with 5 small, meaningful differences (an emoji, a rotated photo, a sticker). Each one shows what it means. 45 s.

### What we agreed

- **What the player sees:** two photos of your room side by side: a teen's wall and shelf with photos of the group, a friendship bracelet, stickers and notes. The differences are meaningful details (the bracelet missing, a photo changed, a heart drawn on a note…); when you find one, a short text explains what it means ("you kept the bracelet she made you").
- **What the player does:** click a difference on either photo; it gets circled on both. A click where there's no difference costs a heart (3 hearts).
- **The social twist:** when you find a difference, the friend it's about sends you a message: find the bracelet and Mia writes "you still have it?? I made that in 6th grade"; find the changed photo and Leo says "that's from the beach day!!". Small things mean a lot to others too.
- **How you win:** find all 5 differences.
- **How you lose:** the 45 seconds run out, or you make 3 wrong clicks.
- **How long it lasts:** 45 seconds at most.
