## The idea

### From the brief

The game asks you to really switch off your phone, handwrite a note to someone you admire and keep it. At the end you can photograph the note. No way to lose.

### What we agreed

- **What the player sees:** only a message on a black screen: "Grab a paper and a pen and write a letter to someone you love."
- **What the player does:** you put the game down and write a real letter by hand; after 20 seconds a quiet "I wrote it" button lets you go on.
- **The social twist:** the twist is the real world: the letter you write and give.
- **How you win:** you come back and press "I wrote it".
- **How you lose:** there is no way to lose.
- **How long it lasts:** as long as you need (the button appears after 20 seconds).

## Decisions

- 01/11/2026: Helena: only a message on a black screen asking you to grab paper and a pen and write a letter to someone you love (rejected: holding to switch off the phone against notifications, and writing a note in the game).
- 01/11/2026: Helena played it: there must be a clear way to go on once the letter is written. The button now says "I wrote it, keep playing" and appears after 4 seconds instead of 20.
